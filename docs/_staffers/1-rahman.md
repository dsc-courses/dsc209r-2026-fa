Tauhidur Rahman is an assistant professor in the Halıcıoğlu Data Science
Institute at UC San Diego, where he directs the Mobile Sensing and Ubiquitous
Computing Laboratory (MOSAIC Lab). His research builds ubiquitous and mobile
health sensing technologies that capture low-level physical signals — acoustic
and electromagnetic waves from our bodies and the surrounding environment — and
map them to biological and behavioral measurements. His work has received best
paper awards at ACM Digital Health in 2016 and ACM IMWUT in 2021, and he was a
Google PhD Fellow in mobile computing.
